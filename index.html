WHO WE ARE

V3R DEVELOPMENT TEAM

Founded on November 7, 2024 by Ray (@rays_multimedia) and brought along with Ethan (@EthanFox17)

An indie development team, driven by Passion and Imagination in creating games, animating, interactive experience, and more.

We primarily build, script, and animate on ROBLOX, and some other programming such as websites and other side projects.

The development team is composed entirely of close friends (handpicked), who share a passion on imagining and creating ideas into innovation, and support one another throughout their journey.
