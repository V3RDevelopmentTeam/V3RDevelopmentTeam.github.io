Introduction

The Sylrysa Project, a solo game project of Sylrysa Nuclear Power Plant by Ray (@XNewXDev) and collaboration game project of PARANORMALITY by V3R Development Team. The Sylrysa Project consists several subprojects or minor projects that may or may not included in both games.

The Major Projects, the games including, Sylrysa Nuclear Power Plant and PARANORMALITY.

Sylrysa Nuclear Power Plant

Sylrysa Nuclear Power Plant (SRY:NPP) is owned by multi-million dollar company, Sylrysa Corporation, founded by Sylrysa Gajiko and consisting its board members are mostly her loyal supporters and others. SRY:NPP is under the management of Sylrysa Corporation.

Sylrysa Storyline, however is not canon to Neutra Lore and will be served as Alternate Universe (AU) to it.
